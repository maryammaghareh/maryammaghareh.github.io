Research profile

From delivery systems
to brain biology.

Maryam Maghareh, PharmD, is a doctoral researcher and research assistant at West Virginia University. Her work connects responsive drug-delivery platforms with questions in mitochondrial function, neurovascular biology, and neurodegeneration.

01

Responsive nanomedicine

pH-responsive and stimuli-responsive nanoparticles for intracellular payload release.

02

Mitochondrial biology

NEET proteins, oxidative stress, iron regulation, and ferroptosis in disease.

03

Neuropharmacology

Mechanisms and therapeutic targets in neurodegeneration and traumatic brain injury.

04

Neurovascular systems

Blood–brain barrier function, microglial morphology, and cerebrovascular disorders.
