Current researchSpring 2025–present

Neurovascular injury & repair

Huber Lab · West Virginia University
Mentor: Jason Huber, PhD

I investigate cellular and molecular responses to brain injury using neuronal cell cultures and rodent models of intracerebral hemorrhage and middle cerebral artery occlusion.

My work includes evaluating therapeutic compounds, analyzing brain tissue, and assessing motor and functional outcomes.

  • Neuronal cell culture
  • Histology & immunohistochemistry
  • Western blotting
  • Behavioral assessment
Research foundationSpring 2022–spring 2023

Formulating drug-delivery systems

Bobbala Lab · West Virginia University
Mentor: Sharan Bobbala, PhD

I developed hydratable mixed-micelle formulations and studied their size, surface charge, encapsulation efficiency, stability, cellular uptake, and toxicity.

  • Micelle formulation
  • Light scattering
  • Confocal microscopy
  • Flow cytometry
